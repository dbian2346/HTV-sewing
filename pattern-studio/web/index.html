        A QUICK PRINT CHECK

        Good patterns start
        with the right scale.

        1. Choose A4 or US Letter to match your paper.
        2. Download the PDF and print the first page at 100% / Actual Size. Disable Fit or Shrink.
        3. Measure the calibration square: it must be 50 × 50 mm between line centers.
        4. Print the remaining pages at the same scale. Trim the white margins and align the repeated crosses in the 10 mm overlap.
        5. Assemble each piece by row and column, then cut along the solid line. Cut every labeled fabric piece once on a single layer.

        SVG is for previewing and vector editing. Use the tiled PDF for printing on standard paper.
